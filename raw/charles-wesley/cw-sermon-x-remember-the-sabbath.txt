First.—The command ofkeeping the sabbath

I dispute not here whether or

The authority by which

it was the supreme

He it was, who declared again and again,

Now who but the Almighty God hath power

seeing therefore that

power to repeal it; and
till he repeals

it

An incontrovertible reason for their obedi

But this is not the

S

'.
In these words, three things are plainly af

days, he ceased from his work upon the seventh

longing to

We perceive here the chief reason of this

Another end of this command God hath de
clared, both on Mount Sinai and long after by

That these sacred

Now as they were instructed by what means

The sum of what has been hitherto observed

Thirdly ;—it is of equal concern to man to

I

To keep either

By saying this day or this

To keep the sabbath- day holy, as well in

is
